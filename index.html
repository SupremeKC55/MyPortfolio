Responsiveness

This portfolio is designed to be responsive on desktops, tablets, and mobile devices. CSS Flexbox and Grid are used to organize the content, while media queries change the layout for smaller screens.

On larger screens, the projects are displayed in multiple columns and the navigation appears horizontally. On smaller screens, the projects change to a single-column layout, the navigation becomes vertical, and the About Me section is arranged vertically. Images and other content also adjust to fit the available screen width.
